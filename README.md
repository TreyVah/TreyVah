## 🚀 About me

I'm a final-year AI & Machine Learning student at ISBAT University in Kampala, Uganda. I build credit risk and fintech systems for African lending institutions, mostly in Python on the backend.

- 🔭 Working on **RiskNova3**, a rebuild of my credit scoring platform for SME lending. It predicts application-stage charge-off risk and explains each score with SHAP.
- 🌱 Also building **StreakFit**, a workout streak platform (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, JWT auth with rotating refresh tokens).
- 🎓 BSc Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning @ ISBAT University
- 🌍 Based in Kampala, Uganda
- 📫 Reach me at [trevormesach23@gmail.com](mailto:trevormesach23@gmail.com) or through my [portfolio](https://treyvah.github.io)
